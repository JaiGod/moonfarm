Life on Tranquil Basin

Hydroponic farming

7 crops from quick lettuce to luxury moonberries. Every dome has 4 trays, and growing plants also make oxygen.

Life support

Balance O2, water, power and food. Electrolyzers drink water, recyclers melt ice, and batteries carry you through the long lunar night.

Build a base

8 module types on an 8x4 grid, each upgradeable to level 3: solar, batteries, recyclers, quarters, rover bays, labs and more.

A crew with feelings

Four astronauts with perks that switch on when morale is high. Vary the menu, hold movie nights, let them call home.

Storms & rockets

Dust storms choke your solar panels and park the rovers. Supply rockets land every sol with changing prices for your crates.

10-mission story

From First Sprouts to the Harvest Festival, guided by Mission Control. Research 8 upgrades in the lab along the way.

How to play

  1. Tap the greenhouse, pick a seed and plant the trays. Tap again when crops are ripe to harvest.
  2. Watch the top bar. If O2, water or power runs out, the base is in trouble.
  3. Tap an empty tile to build. Tap a module to upgrade it or recycle it.
  4. Send rovers for ice (water) and regolith (building material), and sell crates when a rocket is docked.
  5. Finish all 10 missions to throw the Lunar Harvest Festival. If O2 or morale stays at zero too long, the mission is lost.

Controls

Mouse / touchTap tiles to select, build, plant and harvest. Use the side (or bottom, on phones) panel and its tabs.
KeyboardWASD/arrows move the cursor · Enter act · Q/E cycle options · U upgrade · H harvest all · 1-5 tabs · Space pause · F speed · M sound · Esc menu
